# Local forest branch art candidate

One closed tapered centered-Z branch replaces the visual idea of a mechanical cylinder, not the 15-branch layout or growth system. Creative procedural geometry authored with Codex assistance; no upstream mesh, scan, paid asset, texture or download. Not a historical or peach-tree species reconstruction.

FBX contains one mesh, one UV0 and one simple neutral preview material. Two PNGs are Blender shape reviews only, not UE real-shadow or gameplay acceptance. SOURCE.json records actual Blender mesh counts, closed manifold/volume/axis/UV checks and a fresh FBX read. build_branch.py is the reproducible source; no .blend is necessary. Future UE material/import and actual contact-to-forest validation remain pending.
